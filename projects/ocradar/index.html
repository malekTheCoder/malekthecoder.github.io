Overview

Oral cancer is highly treatable when it's caught early, but the early signs are easy to miss if you aren't a clinician, and most people go unscreened between dental visits. You photograph the inside of your mouth, a CNN running on Azure AI Custom Vision classifies the image at 98% accuracy, and the app tells you in plain language whether it's worth getting looked at.

It's a team project. I joined in 2024 as COO and lead developer for Android and Windows, and I worked on the backend and the computer vision model as well. The Android app is Flutter and Dart; iOS is native Swift and SwiftUI.

The point is to get you to a dentist sooner, not to replace one. It flags what looks worth checking, and a professional takes it from there.
